About the Property

Your home base
in San José

Gaia Retreat is a modern loft-style accommodation in the Sabana neighborhood, ideally located near La Sabana Metropolitan Park — the green lung of San José — and just minutes from the financial district, restaurants, museums, and international airport connections.

The space is designed for guests who appreciate clean lines, natural light, and thoughtful details. Everything you need for a comfortable stay is provided, whether you're visiting for business or leisure.

Hosted by Diego, who is available to assist with local tips, transport recommendations, and anything that makes your stay more comfortable.

Policies & Guidelines

Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in — full refund
  • Cancellation 3–6 days before check-in — 50% refund of the nightly rate
  • Cancellation less than 72 hours before check-in — no refund
  • Early departures are non-refundable
  • No-shows treated as cancellations within 72 hours — non-refundable
  • Refunds issued via original payment method within 7–10 business days

House Rules

  • Check-in from 3:00 PM · Check-out by 11:00 AM
  • Maximum 2 guests unless otherwise agreed in writing
  • No smoking inside the property
  • No unregistered guests or parties
  • Pets allowed on prior request only
  • Commercial filming requires written authorization
  • Quiet hours from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM

Privacy Policy

  • We collect name, contact details, and stay information to process your booking
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties except as required for booking processing
  • Payment information is handled by secure third-party processors and not stored by us
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us
  • Booking platforms have their own independent privacy policies

Booking Terms

  • Full payment collected at the time of booking confirmation
  • Valid government-issued ID required upon check-in
  • Lead guest must be 18 years of age or older
  • Property damage may result in additional charges
  • Gaia Retreat reserves the right to decline bookings at its discretion
  • Costa Rican law governs all accommodation agreements
