About the Property

A complete apartment
in Sabana's core

Gaia Retreat is an entire rental apartment in the Sabana neighborhood — one of San José's most desirable residential areas, steps from La Sabana Metropolitan Park. Whether you're here for business or to explore Costa Rica, you'll have everything you need.

The space sleeps up to 5 guests across two bedrooms (each with a queen bed) plus a sofa bed in the living room. Two full bathrooms ensure everyone is comfortable, and the fully equipped kitchen means you can cook whenever you like.

Building amenities include a shared pool and sauna — rare in this area — plus free on-premises parking. Self check-in via smartlock makes arrival effortless at any hour.

Hosted by Diego, a verified Airbnb Superhost with a 100% response rate and average response time under one hour.

Policies & Guidelines

Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Most reservations are booked under the Flexible – 14 days rate: guests may cancel free of charge up to 14 days before arrival
  • Cancellations made within 14 days of arrival are charged the total price of the reservation
  • A prepayment of the total reservation price is collected within 14 days of arrival
  • Some promotional rates are Non-refundable: the total price is charged at the time of booking and no refund is issued for any cancellation
  • The applicable rate and cancellation terms are clearly displayed at the time of booking on each platform
  • No-shows are treated as cancellations and are non-refundable

House Rules

  • Check-in after 3:00 PM · Check-out before 11:00 AM
  • Maximum 5 guests
  • No smoking inside the property
  • No parties or events
  • Pets allowed on request — charges may apply
  • Quiet hours from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM
  • Commercial filming or photography requires written authorization from the host

Privacy Policy

  • We collect name, contact details, and stay information to process your booking
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties except as required for booking processing
  • Payment information is handled by secure third-party processors and not stored by us
  • Booking platforms (Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, VRBO) have their own independent privacy policies

Booking Terms

  • Full payment collected at time of booking confirmation, or at least 14 days prior to the reservation date
  • Valid government-issued ID required upon check-in
  • Lead guest must be 18 years of age or older
  • Children of all ages are welcome
  • Property damage may result in additional charges
  • Gaia Retreat reserves the right to decline bookings at its discretion
  • Costa Rican law governs all accommodation agreements
